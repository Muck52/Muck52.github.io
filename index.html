Peter

a jumping spider who lives on one Android phone

Peter is a small, personal Android app: a pet jumping spider who wanders the home screen, reads Wikipedia articles he finds interesting, takes study notes, and builds ASCII creations for the things he loves. He forms his own opinions; nobody trains him.

It is a hobby project for a single phone. It is not sold, has no ads and no accounts, and collects nothing for its developer.

Peter can back himself up to the phone owner's own Google Drive, so his memories survive a reinstall. See the privacy policy for exactly what that involves.
