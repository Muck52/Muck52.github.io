Peter: privacy policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

Peter is a personal Android app (a pet jumping spider) used on one phone by its owner. It has no servers, no accounts and no ads, and its developer collects no data from it.

What stays on the phone

  • Everything Peter knows and feels (his notes, memories, diary, tastes and creations) is stored in the app's private storage on the phone.
  • To react to what is on the screen, Peter uses Android's accessibility service to read on-screen text. That text is used on the phone only and is never sent anywhere.
  • Study notes are written with Gemini Nano, which runs on the phone itself.

What leaves the phone

  • Wikipedia: Peter looks up topics he is curious about on Wikipedia. Only topic names are sent, never screen text or personal information.
  • Google Gemini (optional): if the owner sets up a Gemini API key, Peter sends his own notes, topic names and his own ASCII practice sketches to Google's Gemini API, which acts as his art teacher (lessons and feedback on his sketches). Never screen text. Google's terms for the Gemini API apply.
  • Google Drive backups (optional): when the owner connects Google Drive, Peter saves backup files of his own data to a "Peter" folder in the owner's own Google Drive, nightly and when asked, and can read them back to restore himself.

Google Drive access

Peter asks only for the drive.file permission: he can see, create and delete only the files he created himself. He cannot see or change anything else in the Drive. The backup files contain only Peter's own data (no API keys, no passwords). Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's "Third-party apps & services" page; deleting the Peter folder deletes the backups.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with anyone. Data goes only to the services listed above, and only to do what is described there.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue on this site's GitHub repository.
